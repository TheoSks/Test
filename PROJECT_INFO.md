# 📊 Sales Management Dashboard

## 🎯 Project Overview

This is a comprehensive **Sales Management & Analytics Dashboard** designed to help businesses track and visualize their sales performance in real-time.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📈 Sales Analytics
- **Total Revenue Tracking** with growth percentage indicators
- **Monthly Performance Charts** with interactive visualizations
- **Sales Trends Analysis** showing highest and lowest performing months
- **Target Achievement Metrics** with progress indicators

### 🏆 Category Management
- **Top Selling Categories** ranked by performance
- Real-time category sales tracking
- Growth indicators for each category
- Visual category icons for quick identification

### 📋 Order Management
- **Recent Orders Table** with customer information
- Order status tracking (Pending, Canceled, Completed)
- Quick search functionality
- Customer avatars and details

### 💡 Business Intelligence
- **Sales Rate Monitoring** with trend indicators
- **Performance Metrics** for decision making
- **Upgrade Prompts** to boost business growth
- **Growth Rate Calculations** for forecasting
